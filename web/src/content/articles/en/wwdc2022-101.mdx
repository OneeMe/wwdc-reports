## Highlight

> WWDC 2022 keynote releases iOS 16 lock screen redesign, WidgetKit lock screen widgets, Live Activities, Focus filters, macOS Ventura Stage Manager, Passkeys, M2 MacBook Air, watchOS 9 and iPadOS 16 external monitor support.

## Core Content

The main thread of this keynote is to reorganize the entrances that users encounter every day.The entry point for iPhone is the lock screen, the entry point for Mac is window and login, and the entry point for iPad is multitasking and external screen.Rather than making these updates a separate list of features, Apple has them serve the same thing: getting users to their current tasks faster on different devices.

iOS 16 的锁屏变化最明显。过去锁屏主要承载时间、通知和相机入口。新版锁屏允许长按编辑、切换照片风格、调整字体颜色、加入 WidgetKit 小组件，并把通知放到底部。这样一来，壁纸、实时状态和个人 Focus 可以同时存在。

macOS Ventura 的 Stage Manager 处理另一个问题：窗口太多时，当前任务被其他窗口打断。Stage Manager 把当前窗口放在中央，把其他应用缩略排列在左侧。iPadOS 16 也获得 Stage Manager 和外接显示器扩展显示，iPad 开始支持更接近桌面环境的多窗口工作流。

## Detailed Content

### iOS 16 锁屏与实时信息

（[05:10](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/101/?time=310)）Craig Federighi 从锁屏开始介绍 iOS 16。用户长按进入编辑器，滑动尝试不同样式，系统同时调整颜色滤镜、背景和时间字体。照片可以启用深度效果，让人物出现在时间前方。

（[09:06](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/101/?time=546)）开发者通过 WidgetKit 将可扫视信息放到锁屏。演示中包括天气、Activity rings 和日历。锁屏空间有限，适合展示单个数字、状态或下一项事件。

([09:44](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/101/?time=584)) Live Activities are used for lock screen real-time status.Keynote cited three scenarios: NBA scores, Uber trip progress, and fitness data.Now Playing uses the same idea, extending the playback controls into a full-screen view with album art.

### Focus extends into the application

([10:34](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/101/?time=634)) Focus can be bound to the lock screen.Work Focus displays meeting and to-do widgets, and Personal Focus switches to friends and family-related content.

（[11:24](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/101/?time=684)）Focus filters 将 Focus 带进应用。Safari 可以只显示工作标签组，Messages、Mail 和 Calendar 也能按当前 Focus 过滤内容。Apple 明确提到开发者可以使用新 API 在自己的应用中支持类似过滤。

### Messages、SharePlay 和 Shared with You

（[12:20](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/101/?time=740)）Messages 新增编辑已发送消息、撤回最近消息、标记未读三项功能。

([13:20](https://developer.apple.com/videos/play/wwdc2022/101/?time=800)) Shared with You opens new API.Videos, articles or recipes sent by friends through Messages can be waiting for users to view them later in the corresponding application.The discovery portal for SharePlay has also become more straightforward.

### macOS、iPadOS、watchOS 与硬件

（keynote 后半段）macOS Ventura 引入 Stage Manager、Continuity Camera 和 Passkeys。Continuity Camera 让 iPhone 成为 Mac 摄像头。Passkeys 使用设备认证替代密码，并通过 iCloud 钥匙串同步。

M2 使用第二代 5nm 工艺，搭载于重新设计的 MacBook Air 和 13 英寸 MacBook Pro。watchOS 9 新增表盘、跑步指标、房颤历史和用药提醒。iPadOS 16 支持 Stage Manager、外接显示器扩展显示和 Freeform 协作画布。

## Core Takeaways

- **Design real-time status cards for lock screen**: Food delivery, flight, taxi, sports and competition applications can put the most important real-time status on the lock screen.The core design is a small amount of text, a clear progression, and a scannable layout.

- **Let Focus change the scope of application content**: Notes, email, reading, and chat applications can provide work/personal filtering.When a user enters Work Focus, only work accounts, work labels, or work projects are displayed.

- **Turn Messages sharing into in-app to-dos**: Content applications can be connected to Shared with You to display articles, videos or recipes sent by others on the homepage, solving the scenario of "receiving links but not having time to read them".

- **为 iPad 外接屏准备双屏工作流**：编辑类应用可在 iPad 显示工具和导航，在外接显示器显示画布或预览。设计时要考虑窗口缩放、指针输入和键盘快捷键。
